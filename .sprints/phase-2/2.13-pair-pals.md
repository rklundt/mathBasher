# Sprint 2.13: Puzzles subject + Pair Pals (a memory game)

**Phase:** 2
**Status:** In progress (design reviewed)
**Blocks:** Pattern Train (the next puzzle) reuses this sprint's Puzzles flow.
**Depends on:** 2.12 (Released, v2.12.1).
**Estimated effort:** 4–5 days, plus the owner's sounds (optional; borrowed ones ship first).

## Summary

A second subject, **Puzzles**, and its first game: **Pair Pals**, a memory-match game. Cards lie face down; the child turns two over per turn, looking for matching pictures. A match stays face up; a miss turns back over. There are no points, timer or lives: the round counts turns, and the best (fewest turns) is kept per size. Owner: "pair pals and pattern train can be under a new subject of Puzzles … choose Puzzles and then that goes to select games, and from there you select specifics (like difficulty)" (2026-10-25).

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-25)

1. **Flow:** pick the Puzzles subject → the puzzle game (only Pair Pals for now) → a setup screen (theme and size) → play. No Game Select or Difficulty step for puzzles.
2. **Sizes (columns × rows):** Easy 4 × 3 (6 pairs), Medium 5 × 4 (10 pairs), Hard 6 × 5 (15 pairs).
3. **Themes:** dragons, dinosaurs, aliens, cute animals, desserts.
   - Cute animals and desserts are drawn in code, kawaii, like every game since 2.5.4.
   - Dinosaurs, aliens and dragons are the owner's Midjourney art, split into one cut-out per creature: 20 picks each, chosen by the owner from the contact sheets (dinosaurs, aliens and dragons approved 2026-10-25). "Remove the kawaii" for these three.
4. **Smooth card flips.**
5. **No scoring yet:** count turns to win; keep the best turns.
6. **Sounds:** borrow from the cute games (Balance Scale, Penguin Slide, Bunny Garden) to start; per-theme sounds may come later. During the build the owner sent four tile flips (the turn's first card; the second plays Bunny Garden's / Balance Scale's success or fail sound until Pair Pals has its own) and Pair Pals' background music (maybe one per card theme later).
7. **Side space:** a theme mascot beside the board (proposed with the proofs, not objected to).

## Design

### D1. The flow

- **"Pick your challenge!"** gets working subject tabs: **Math** and **Puzzles**. Tapping a tab swaps the buttons below in place, with a quick fade-in. Before this the tabs were labels only (one subject). It opens on the last pick's tab.
- The **Puzzles** tab lists puzzle games as tiles: **Pair Pals** ("Find the matching pairs!") and **Pattern Train** ("Coming soon": dimmed; a tap wiggles it and floats "Coming soon!").
- **Pair Pals → its setup screen** ("Pair Pals: pick your cards"): five theme tiles, each with a sample picture; three size tiles ("Easy", "6 pairs · 4 × 3"); the best for the chosen size ("Your best: 9 turns · Can you do it in fewer?", or "Find all the pairs in as few turns as you can!"); Back and Start. The theme and size are remembered in this browser.
- **Picker → Loading → setup → the board** (Loading comes first so the setup's sample pictures are in memory). Back on the setup screen goes to the picker on the Puzzles tab.
- **Menu Start remembers the last pick of the visit:** a math challenge → Game Select (as today); Pair Pals → its setup screen; nothing yet → the picker (as today).
- Picking a puzzle from the picker opened by a challenge chip still goes to the puzzle's setup (a chip's `next` is for challenges).
- **The pause card stays math-only** for math games. Pair Pals has its own pause card: Resume, Settings, Change Cards (the setup screen), Quit to Menu.

### D2. The board (1280 × 720)

- A soft pastel backdrop per theme, a slim top bar: **Turns: 7** (left), **Pairs: 3 / 10** (middle), mute and pause (right). No "Best" here: a bare number reads like a score to beat upward.
- Cards are square, sized to fit the board area (Easy ≈ 180 px, Medium ≈ 140 px, Hard ≈ 110 px), centred, with a theme mascot in the side space.
- **Card back:** the theme's colour, soft dots and a white star. **Face:** cream with the picture.
- **Flip:** a quick squash to edge-on (≈ 120 ms), swap, open (≈ 120 ms), with a slight lift.
- **A match:** both cards pop, get a green ring and a sparkle, the mascot hops; then the ring fades and the cards dim a little and stay face up.
- **A miss:** both give a tiny wiggle (no ring: most turns are misses), a hold (1200 ms) to remember them, then they flip back. Tapping another card during the hold flips the pair back at once and turns the new card; tapping one of the pair just flips them back. A face-up or matched card ignores taps and costs no turn; taps wait out a flip (240 ms).
- **A turn** = the second card of a pair turned over. Turns and pairs update in the top bar.
- **Win:** the last pair, then every card does a little wave, confetti, and a panel: "You did it!", "You found all 10 pairs in 18 turns!", then "Your first win!", "New best!" or "Your best is 15 turns", and buttons Play Again (same theme and size, a new deal), Change Cards (setup), Menu.
- **Keyboard:** the first arrow shows a yellow focus ring, arrows move it over the cards; Space / Enter turns the focused card; Esc pauses.
- **The first tap** is invited by "👆 Tap two cards to find a match!"; it goes on the first tap.
- The deal: the theme's 20 pictures, the round's pairs picked at random (near look-alikes kept apart when there are enough others: `config.pairPals.twinGroups`), shuffled. Pure and seeded so it's tested.

### D3. Art

- **Code-drawn (`scripts/sprites/pairpals.mjs`, `pnpm sprite:pairpals`):** 20 cute animals and 20 desserts, each its own colour and shape (the proofs' style); the five card backs; the five backdrops and a darkened menu copy.
- **Midjourney themes (the same script):** packs the owner-approved cut-outs (kept in the ignored `.sprite-source/pair-pals/`) into one sheet per theme at the card picture size.
- WebP sheets, 20 frames each (5 × 4), about 200 px a frame.
- `CREDITS.md`: the code-drawn art as before; the three creature sets as the owner's Midjourney art.

### D4. Rules, best turns and telemetry

- No points, no lives, no timer; Pair Pals never touches My Scores' points or the high-score table.
- **Best turns per size** (any theme), saved in this browser (`mathbasher.pairPals`, with the remembered theme and size). Unreadable storage starts fresh, as My Scores does.
- My Scores doesn't show puzzles yet (it's built around points); a later sprint can add a Puzzles row.
- Telemetry: `PuzzlePicked` (`puzzleId`, `source`); `PairPalsStarted` / `PairPalsCompleted` (`theme`, `boardSize`, `turns`, `newBest`), `PairPalsQuit`, `GamePaused` / `GameResumed`.

### D5. Registration (ADR-0011 / ADR-0014)

- `GameId` gains `'pair-pals'`; the math games become `MathGameId`, and math-only code (Difficulty, `questionsPerRoundFor`, Game Over) takes that.
- `SubjectId` gains `'puzzles'`; subjects become challenge subjects (Math) or puzzle subjects (Puzzles, listing `PuzzleId`s).
- Scene keys `PairPalsSetup` and `PairPals`; `GAME_BG_MAP`, music (the owner's, one per card theme: `PAIR_PALS_MUSIC`, loaded on demand), midground (none), audio scopes, Loading caption.

## Stories

### Story 1: Design review (playability, cuteness, architecture)
**Done (2026-10-25).**
**Kid UX / cuteness:** taken — "Change Cards" (not "New Cards": Play Again already deals new cards); misses feel normal (no orange ring, a tiny wiggle; most turns are misses); the miss hold 1200 ms, a tap skips it; matched cards settle (the ring fades, the card dims a little) so the face-down ones stand out; input rules (a face-up or matched card ignores taps and costs nothing; one card at a time; tapping one of the missed pair just flips them back); no near-twin creatures in one deal (twin groups per theme, the deal avoids them); a first-tap hint ("Tap two cards to find a match!"); "best" reads as fewer ("Your best: 9 turns · Can you do it in fewer?", "Your first win!", "New best!"), and no "Best" on the board's top bar; the picker opens on the last pick's tab, the tabs' content fades in on a swap; the "Coming soon" tile wiggles when tapped; size tiles lead with Easy / Medium / Hard; first visit: Cute animals + Easy. Not taken yet: a match tray, a peek at the start, a deal animation, best per theme, tab icons (emoji render differently by device), filled tabs (the selected tab is ringed, as every choice is).
**Owner (2026-10-25), sounds:** the first card of a turn plays one of the owner's four tile flips; the second card plays the result: Bunny Garden's and Balance Scale's success or fail sounds (the owner is making Pair Pals' own success sounds). Then the owner sent Pair Pals' background music.
**Architecture:** taken — `MathGameId` (the nine) and `PuzzleGameId` (`'pair-pals'`), `GameId` their union; math-only code takes `MathGameId`, Difficulty guards a puzzle id; Game Over's `gameId` required; subjects a union (challenge subjects and puzzle subjects), `CHALLENGE_SUBJECTS` for My Scores, `subjectOf` and the pause card (My Scores' "one subject starts open" rule counts challenge subjects); puzzles listed in `core/puzzles.ts`; borrowed sounds load for Pair Pals too (`alsoFor` on audio entries); picker → Loading → setup (its pictures are loaded first) → board; `Settings.lastPick` (in memory) with a pure, tested `menuStartTarget`; `PauseOverlay` reused with a puzzle variant and its own destinations; `config.pairPals` in core; `PairPalsProgress` on `storage.ts` like My Scores; pure, seeded, tested deal / turn / layout / focus.
