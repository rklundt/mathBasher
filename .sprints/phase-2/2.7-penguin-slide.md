## Summary

A cute, snowy game. A kawaii penguin slides down a hill on a little sled toward its igloo. Each question sends a row of round, smiling fish up the hill toward it, one per lane, each carrying an answer. **Tap the right fish**: the penguin swoops over and gulps it. The wrong "fish" is a snowball: **BONK!** It's the first game where the player character visibly changes lanes. One tap per question, so even the youngest players can manage it.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-16)

- **Controls: one tap per question.** Tap a fish, or anywhere in its lane. The penguin glides to that lane and catches it at once; there's no counting taps and no waiting.
  - Extras for older kids: the arrow keys move one lane and Space / Enter grabs; number keys 1–5 jump to a lane; on touch, drag the penguin sideways and let go to grab.
- **Right fish:** gulp, sparkle, points, the next question.
- **Wrong fish:** it's a snowball. BONK, the penguin spins, a life is lost, and the same question comes back with a fresh row.
- **Time's up:** the row is the visible countdown. If it reaches the penguin uncaught, the snowballs bonk it and it tumbles: a life is lost and the same question comes back. (Matches Frog Splash and Space Shooter.)
- **Lives:** 3 per round. **Questions:** 12. **Finish:** the penguin slides into the igloo, and the family cheers.
- **Difficulty:** Easy / Medium / Hard = **3 / 4 / 5 lanes**, plus the shared time per question (25 / 18 / 12 s, stretched for harder math).
- **Characters:**
  - **Penguins:** three kawaii colorways, picked at random each round like the frogs and axolotls. Classic (charcoal), Blueberry (round navy with a head tuft) and Fluffy chick (gray down). All drawn in code, with thick outlines, big shiny eyes and blush, after the owner's reference sheet (kept private in `.sprite-source/raw/penguin-slide/`).
  - **Fish:** round, front-facing, two eyes, blush and a smile, in four or more colors. The answer rides on a dark badge, so long fractions stay readable.
- **Scene:** a snowy hill drawn in code (sky, distant mountains, pines, lane marks, the igloo at the top). The owner may enhance it later.
- **Audio:**
  - **Music:** the owner's loop, shipped as `music/loop-8.mp3`.
  - **To come from the owner:** gulp, bonk (also used for time's up) and a lane-change swish. Optionally a sliding-on-snow loop as the background layer, and a finish cheer.

## Stories

### Story 1: Design review (gate)
Settle the open questions, run the review panel on the plan, then get the owner's approval before building:
- **Game Select:** it is three rows of two today. Decide how a seventh tile fits on a phone, for example a 4 + 3 layout or a scrolling grid.
- **Swoop timing:** how fast the penguin crosses lanes on a tap, whether a second tap mid-swoop is allowed, and whether the row keeps moving during the swoop.
- **Pure, tested rules for each outcome:** caught right, caught wrong, time's up, last life. Use the `resolveBubbleEvent` pattern from 2.6.3, plus lane geometry (fish row position from time left, tap to lane, lanes per difficulty).
- **Shared code:** what can come straight from the existing helpers (`callouts`, `roundRules` / `QuestionClock`, `GameSceneLifecycle`, the pause and Game Over screens) and what is new.
- **The new-game checklist** in ADR-0011 (scene key, game id, the dispatch maps, asset scope, music map, HUD, playtest list).
- **Art plan:** the generator scripts (penguin sheet with three colorways and poses; fish sheet; snowball; hill and igloo), frame sizes, and new filenames (the week-long asset cache).
