## Summary

A cute, snowy game. A kawaii penguin sledges down a long hill toward its igloo, far away at the bottom. The camera sits just behind the penguin, so the penguin is near the bottom of the screen and the hill runs away to the horizon. Each question sends a row of round, smiling fish toward the penguin from down the hill, one fish per lane, each carrying an answer. **Tap the fish with the right answer**: the penguin swoops over and gulps it. A wrong "fish" turns out to be a snowball: **BONK!** It's the first game where the player character visibly changes lanes. It takes one tap per question, so even the youngest players can manage it.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-16)

- **Controls:** one tap per question. Tap a fish, or anywhere in its lane on the hill. The penguin glides over and catches it; there's no counting taps and no waiting. Keyboard: ← / → choose a lane, Space grabs, and 1–5 pick a lane directly.
- **Right fish:** gulp, sparkle, points, next question.
- **Wrong fish:** it's a snowball. BONK, a short spin, a life lost, and the same question again with a fresh row.
- **Time's up:** the row reaches the penguin, it tumbles (BONK), and a life is lost. The same question comes back. This matches Frog Splash and Space Shooter.
- **Round:** 3 lives, 12 questions.
- **Difficulty:** Easy / Medium / Hard = **3 / 4 / 5 fish per row**, with the shared time per question (25 / 18 / 12 s, stretched for harder math).
- **Characters:**
  - **Penguins:** three kawaii colorways, one picked at random each round: Classic (charcoal), Blueberry (round, navy, with a tuft) and Fluffy (a gray chick). They're drawn in code after the owner's reference sheet, which stays private in `.sprite-source/raw/penguin-slide/`.
  - **Fish:** round, facing forward, with two eyes, blush and a smile, in five colours. The answer sits on a dark badge.
- **Scene:** a snowy hill drawn in code. The owner may enhance it later.
- **Audio:**
  - **Music:** the owner's loop, `loop-8`.
  - **From the owner, after seeing the game:** gulp, bonk (also used for time's up) and swish. Optional: a sliding-on-snow loop and a finish cheer.

## Design review (Story 1)

The draft was checked against the code by the full review panel. InfoSec and DevOps passed it. Architecture, senior dev, support and legal asked for changes; none needed a redesign. Everything below includes their findings. The main corrections:

- **Hard needs 5 answers.** Questions come with 4 choices, so the row tops them up with `extendChoices`, like Space Shooter.
- **No narrowing lanes.** Narrowing toward the horizon would make far taps land in the wrong lane.
- **Badges stay readable.** The answer badge has a minimum size, so far fish stay readable on phones.
- **Keyboard grab is Space only.** Enter also toggles the HUD's Mute.
- **No grab on Resume.** A Space press that resumes from Pause must not also grab.
- **Shared rules.** The life-and-retry rules and the answer picking become shared code, not a third copy.
- **Game Select layout.** It needs one that keeps big tap targets.
- **Credits.** `loop-8` gets a credits entry.
- **Plus:** a fuller checklist, explicit asset scopes and size budgets, and telemetry.

## Design

### D1. Game Select: seven tiles in a 4 + 3 grid
- **Grid:** four tiles on the top row and three centred below. Each tile is **280 × 120**, the same height as today's, so it still gives about 54–65 px tap targets on a phone. The subtitle can wrap to two lines inside it, which `PlaceholderButton` is built for.
- **Width:** 4 × 280 + 3 × 14 = 1162 of 1280, so it fits.
- **Code:** `tile()`'s column and row types widen; the keyboard order runs row by row, then Back.
- **New tile:** "Penguin Slide", subtitle "Slide down and catch the right fish."
- **Check:** a screenshot at 844 × 390 (iPhone Safari landscape, no fullscreen). If the subtitles crowd, the fallback is 3 columns of 290 × 120 (3 + 3 + 1).

### D2. Hill, lanes, row and swoop
- **Lanes:** 3 / 4 / 5 straight lanes of equal width across the hill, with no narrowing. Depth comes only from the fish growing as they approach (about 0.7× far, 1.0× near) and from the hill art. The penguin's lane is marked with a soft ring under it (shape, not colour), which helps keyboard players.
- **The row's approach is the countdown.**
  - The row's y comes straight from the question clock: `rowProgress(remainingSec, totalSec)` gives 0 when it appears and 1 when time is up.
  - The picture and the timer can't disagree.
  - The clock only advances when the scene ticks it, so pausing freezes the row, the swoop and the banners, as in Frog Splash.
- **Answer badges:** the dark pill from Space Shooter, scaled with `badgeScale(progress)`, never smaller than about 30 px of text (about 14 px on a phone). A test checks that neighbouring badges never overlap at 5 lanes with the longest fraction.
- **A tap:**
  - **What counts:** taps only count inside the hill's tap window (`config.penguinSlide.tapWindow`), never on the HUD bar or the sky. A tap on a fish picks that fish; otherwise the tap's x picks the lane (`laneAt(x, lanes)`; straight lanes make this exact at any height).
  - **On the tap:**
    - stop the clock;
    - set `busy`;
    - decide the outcome (D3);
    - squish the tapped fish and lean the penguin, so the tap feels instant.
  - **The swoop:**
    - the penguin glides across to the lane (`swoopMs`, about 250 ms), then up to the fish (`reachMs`);
    - the outcome plays when it arrives: gulp, or snowball reveal + BONK;
    - lives and score change at that moment, so the heart drops with the bonk, as Frog Splash does when its frog lands.
  - **Back:** the penguin returns to the catch line during the pause before the next row (`returnMs`). The next row waits for it.
- **Ignored taps:** taps are ignored from the tap until the new row has entered plus `inputLockMs` (about 250 ms). A fast second tap after a BONK can't cost another life.
- **Keyboard:**
  - **Lanes:** ← / → move the target lane at once, with a swish, and restart the glide on rapid presses. 1–5 jump to a lane; numbers above the lane count do nothing. A shared `NUMBER_KEYS` constant in `src/game/ui/` adds `FIVE`.
  - **Grab:** Space grabs the target lane.
  - **When ignored:** all of these are ignored while `busy`, and **on the frame the game resumes from Pause**. The Space that pressed Resume must not also grab.
  - **No Enter:** Enter isn't used, because it also activates the HUD's Mute button.
- **Time's up:**
  - **Effect:** the row reaches the penguin uncaught, the snowballs bonk it and it tumbles. "Out of time!" shows, even on the last life, as in Frog Splash.
  - **The right fish:** it's never auto-caught; it swims past and pops, so a kid doesn't learn that every fish is a trap.
  - **Retry:** the clock restarts at full time with a fresh row.
- **Wrong catch:** a snow puff and dizzy stars, a 400–600 ms spin, no screen shake, then "Bonk! Try again!" (just "Bonk!" on the last life). The same question comes back with full time and a fresh row.
- **Finish:**
  - After 12 correct answers, the penguin slides into the igloo: the cheer pose and a "You made it!" banner for about 1.8 s, then Game Over.
  - There's no penguin family this sprint (no sprites for it); it's on the backlog.
  - Game Over's pass and stars are set the way Frog Splash sets them: **pass** means reaching the igloo, and **stars** are based on how many questions were answered before the round ended, so running out of lives on question 10 isn't reported as a pass.
- **Tap hint:** "👆 Tap the fish with the right answer!" (it matches Space Shooter's hint, and "the right fish" could be read as "the fish on the right"). It sits above the penguin, clear of the row's path, and goes on the first tap.
- **Fair fish:** every item looks like a fish until it's caught. The fish colours and wiggle frames are chosen without looking at the answer (tested), so colour is never a clue.

### D3. Shared rules + Penguin Slide rules (pure, tested)
**Shared, in `src/game/systems/roundRules.ts`:**
- `resolveRetryEvent(state, event)` is the "a wrong pick or a time-out costs a life and retries the same question" rule. Frog Splash and Space Shooter run it inline today; they adopt it later (backlog).
  - **State:** `{ correctAnswer, livesUsed, maxLives, usedWrong }`.
  - **Event:** `{ type: 'pick', answer }` or `{ type: 'timeout' }`.

| Result | Carries |
|---|---|
| `correct` | `record` (`usedWrongShot` = the flag) |
| `wrong` / `timeout` | `livesUsed`, `livesRemaining`, `usedWrong: true` |
| `out-of-lives` | `livesUsed`, `livesRemaining: 0`, `cause: 'wrong' \| 'timeout'`, `record { wasCorrect: false, usedWrongShot: true }` |

  It uses `resolveStrike`, and the scene switches on the result with an `assertNever` default. `OutcomeRecord` moves here from `mathALotlMath.ts`. Math-a-lotl keeps its own rules, because its time-outs cost no life.
- `pickRowAnswers(question, count, rng)` puts the right answer in a random slot and fills the rest with wrong choices. It calls `extendChoices` when the question has fewer choices than slots. Frog Splash's `rowAnswers` and Space Shooter's `shipAnswers` are identical, so both switch to this in this sprint, with tests.

**`src/game/systems/penguinSlideMath.ts`:**
- `laneCenters(count, left, right)`
- `laneAt(x, lanes)`, clamped to the lanes that exist
- `rowProgress(remainingSec, totalSec)`: 0 at the start, 1 at and after time-out, never backwards
- `rowY(progress, topY, catchY)`
- `fishScale(progress)`
- `badgeScale(progress)` (with a floor)
- `pickFishLooks(count, colourCount, rng)` (independent of the answer)
- `inTapWindow(x, y, window)`

Tests cover the end values, midpoints, clamping, every lane count, the badge floor and no-overlap, and the lane counts matching the Difficulty subtitles.

**Lives:** `usedWrong` resets only when the next question starts, never on a retry. A correct answer after a wrong one or a time-out scores half.

### D4. Reuse, config and telemetry
- **Reused:**
  - `QuestionClock` + `questionSeconds`
  - `GameSceneLifecycle` (HUD; pause with Change Game; Game Over; listener clean-up on shutdown)
  - `RoundController`
  - `callouts` (tap hint, float note, screen banner)
  - the sparkle burst
- **`config.penguinSlide`:**
  - `maxLives`
  - `speed: { slow: { fishCount: 3 }, medium: { fishCount: 4 }, fast: { fishCount: 5 } }` (no per-game countdown; the shared time per question applies)
  - the hill and lane geometry and the tap window
  - `swoopMs`, `reachMs`, `returnMs`, `inputLockMs`
  - the fish scales and the badge floor
  - banner and note timings
- **Difficulty screen:** "3 fish · 25s". The heading reads "Difficulty"; `speedSectionTitleFor` becomes an exhaustive switch, so a new game can't silently fall back to "Speed".
- **Telemetry:**
  - **Events:** `QuestionStarted` / `QuestionEnded`, plus `PenguinSlide.wrong`, `.timeout`, `.outOfLives` and `.finished`.
  - **Props:** they use `questionProps()` with the existing allowed property names only. No lane index, tap position or input method.

## Playtest (new mode)
**Layout and fairness:**
- [ ] Game Select at 844 × 390: all seven tiles readable and comfortably tappable; keyboard order runs row by row.
- [ ] Difficulty shows "Difficulty" and "3 fish · 25s" / "4 fish · 18s" / "5 fish · 12s".
- [ ] Taps: a far fish in each lane, at 3, 4 and 5 lanes, picks that lane. HUD, pause and mute taps never count as an answer.
- [ ] Far badges are readable on a phone, including long fractions.
- [ ] Fish colour never gives the answer away.

**Outcomes:**
- [ ] Right fish: gulp, sparkle, points, next question.
- [ ] Wrong fish:
  - the snowball reveal, spin and "Bonk! Try again!"; one life lost; the same question with a fresh row;
  - a fast double tap after a BONK costs nothing.
- [ ] Time's up:
  - tumble and "Out of time!"; one life lost;
  - the right fish swims past (it isn't caught).
- [ ] Last life: "Bonk!" or "Out of time!", then "Out of lives!", then Game Over (not a pass).
- [ ] Finish: the penguin reaches the igloo, "You made it!", then Game Over.

**Controls:**
- [ ] ← / → move the lane with the ring; Space grabs; 1–5 jump to a lane (4–5 do nothing at 3 lanes).
- [ ] Enter doesn't toggle Mute.
- [ ] Space on Resume doesn't grab.

**Pause and Change Game:**
- [ ] Pause mid-row, mid-swoop and mid-banner: everything freezes and resumes.
- [ ] Switching tabs pauses the clock.
- [ ] Pause → Change Game keeps the math type and difficulty.

**Look and sound:**
- [ ] All three penguins stand out on the snow (especially Fluffy's gray).
- [ ] Sound levels are comfortable on headphones.
