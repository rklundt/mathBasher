## Summary

A sixth game mode with a first-person framing: the kid sits **inside a spaceship cockpit**, looking out of the window at space. Alien ships carrying the answers fly around in the window; tap (or click) the ship with the right answer and the cockpit's lasers blast it.

Three layers, back to front:

- **The view** — the cosmos image, full screen.
- **The ships** — 3 / 4 / 5 alien ships (by difficulty), each with the answer in a dark porthole in its middle.
- **The cockpit** — a full-screen cockpit image with a see-through window. Only taps inside the window count.

How it differs from the other modes: Alien Shoot has falling lanes and a FIRE button; Asteroid Field has aim + fire; here the kid taps the target directly, first-person.

## Game rules (decided 2026-10-13)

1. **12 questions per round.** Each question warps in a fresh wave of ships.
2. **Flight:** ships fly curvy paths (their turn rate wanders), bounce off the window frame and off each other, and always stay fully visible in the window. Harder = faster.
3. **Input:** tap a ship, or press 1–5 (ships left to right). Lasers fire from the cockpit's two cannons.
4. **Right ship:** it explodes, the other ships warp away, next question.
5. **Wrong ship:** its shield blocks the lasers ("Shields up! Try again!"), it warps away, one life is lost; the question stays with the remaining ships (half points once solved).
6. **Time runs out:** the ships blast the cockpit (red bolts, cracks on the glass, red flash, shake — "Out of time! You got blasted!"), one life is lost, and the same question comes back with fresh ships.
7. **3 lives per round;** the last one ends the round ("Out of lives!").
8. **Difficulty:** Easy 3 ships · 20 s, Medium 4 ships · 15 s, Hard 5 ships · 12 s, flying faster as it gets harder; times stretch for harder math (`round.mathTimeScale`). Questions carry 4 answer choices, so Hard's 5th ship gets one more plausible wrong answer (a nearby whole number, or another common fraction slip).
9. **Scoring:** shared scoring (half points on a question that needed a retry); stars by correct answers, like the other arcade modes.

## Assets

- **Ships:** 20 cartoon alien ships drawn in code — 10 hull shapes (saucer, rocket, beetle, manta, ring, crab, jellyfish, X-fighter, squid, drone) × 2 looks — with a dark porthole for the answer and a soft halo so they read on dark space. Source: `scripts/sprites/alienships.mjs` → `sprites/hero/alien-ships.webp`.
- **Answer labels:** white with a dark outline; wide answers (mixed numbers like "1 11/12") shrink a little and sit on a dark rounded "display" pill so they stay readable over any hull colour.
- **Cockpit + view:** two owner-supplied images, processed by `scripts/sprites/cockpit.mjs` (sources kept in the private source folder) → `sprites/hero/cockpit.webp` (1.5× the design canvas), `sprites/bg/space-cosmos.webp`, and the dimmed menu backdrop `sprites/bg/space-shooter.webp`. `--measure` prints the window opening the ship bounds are based on.
- **Audio:** the owner's music loop (`music/loop-7.mp3`, generated in ElevenLabs from the owner's prompts); Alien Shoot's laser, hit and wrong sounds, and the timeout sound.
