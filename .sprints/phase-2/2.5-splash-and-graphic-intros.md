# Sprint 2.5: Splash screen, graphic intros + hero selection

**Phase:** 2
**Status:** Planned
**Closed:** —

**Blocks:** Nothing — presentation polish + a character-select feature. Phase 3 can proceed in parallel.
**Depends on:** 2.2.1 (post-Climb cleanup) — best landed after 2.2.1 so the WebP pipeline + the touched scenes are stable. Not a hard dependency.

## Summary

The app's "front door", per-game intros, and player-character all need a pass. Boot currently goes through a minimal DOM click-to-start splash straight into the menu; picking a game mode drops the kid into gameplay with no intro beat; and there's no way for a kid to pick a hero that looks like them. This sprint makes the first impressions — and the player's sense of ownership — match the quality of the games themselves.

Two on-theme title images already exist (Midjourney, prompted by Ray), surfaced during sprint 2.2's audit-cleanup and currently unreferenced:
- `Title-Splash-1.png` — kid + alien + blaster, arcade-hero pose.
- `Title-Splash-2.png` — kid leaping with a blaster, alien companion, floating math symbols.

The sprint begins with a **design discussion** to settle the shape, then builds. The "improved graphic intros" half (per-game-mode intro beat) is scoped lighter and may grow its own stories once the design is settled.

## Stories

### Story 1: Design discussion — splash + intro shape

**Type:** Design (settle before building)

**Context:** Open questions to resolve before any code. No output beyond a decision record appended to this story.

**Questions to settle:**
- **Boot splash placement** — replace the current DOM click-to-start splash, or add a new Phaser scene between `BootScene` and `MenuScene`?
- **Static vs animated** — still image, or animated (parallax layers, particle drift, a small hero idle loop)?
- **Which art** — `Title-Splash-1`, `Title-Splash-2`, or both (e.g. one as the boot splash, one as a menu backdrop)?
- **Wordmark** — composite the "mathBasher" title over the art, or is the title baked into the image?
- **Start affordance** — a button, "tap anywhere", or auto-advance after N seconds?
- **`?autostart` interaction** — the dev shortcut must still bypass whatever the splash becomes.
- **Per-game intro beat** — when the kid picks a game mode, is there a short intro card / animation (mode name + a one-line "how to play" + the mode's hero) before the round starts? How long, skippable how?
- **Consistency** — should the three game modes share one intro template, or each get bespoke treatment?

**Output:** a decision record appended here; the build stories below get their specifics filled in from it.

### Story 4: Player hero selection — 4 diverse hero options

**Type:** Design + code + assets

**Context:** Let the player pick their hero from **4 options** chosen for representation — one each of: female / dark-skinned, female / light-skinned, male / dark-skinned, male / light-skinned. Every kid should be able to see themselves in the character they play. Fits this sprint because hero-select is a front-of-game presentation beat alongside the splash + intros.

**Design questions to settle first** (the games' current heroes are not all the same KIND — this needs a decision before building):
- **Which hero does the choice drive?** Number Climb's climber is a human character (today a procedural placeholder) — the 4 diverse human heroes map onto it directly. Alien Shoot + Asteroid Field heroes are spaceSHIPS (speeders / asteroid-heroes), where a "female/male, dark/light" human selection doesn't apply. Options: (a) the selection drives the Climb climber only; (b) the 4 heroes appear as the PILOT composited into/onto the ships for the arcade modes; (c) a universal player avatar shown on menus / splash / GameOver, separate from the in-game hero sprites. Decide before building.
- **Where is the picker?** A dedicated hero-select screen (when? first run? always reachable from the menu?), or a row on an existing screen?
- **Persistence** — the choice is saved (localStorage, mirroring the audience/settings pattern) so it sticks across sessions; a sensible default for first-run.

**Output (after the design is settled):**
- 4 hero images delivered (Midjourney) covering the female/male × dark/light matrix; processed through the sprite pipeline (WebP — `hero` kind, lossless per the sprint-2.2.1 story-6 convention).
- A hero-select UI — the player picks one of the 4; the choice persists.
- The selection is applied per the design decision (Climb climber and/or arcade pilots and/or menu avatar).
- CREDITS.md entry for the 4 new hero images.
- Telemetry: which hero is chosen (useful representation-engagement signal).

**Acceptance:**
- The player can pick from 4 visibly diverse hero options; the choice persists across sessions.
- The chosen hero is reflected wherever the design decision says it applies.
- A sensible first-run default.
- CREDITS.md updated; tests stay green.
