## Summary

First time the project lets the kid pick a hero. The 4 Midjourney-generated heroes cover the female/male × dark/light-skin matrix so every kid can see themselves in the character they play. Purely cosmetic — the chosen hero appears as the MenuScene avatar + the GameOver banner; in-game gameplay sprites are untouched (Alien Shoot keeps its Speeders, Climb keeps its `heroSkin` picker, Asteroid Field keeps its asteroid-heroes round-robin).

Picker shows on first visit (full-screen, hard-gated — no Skip). After the kid picks, the choice persists via localStorage; subsequent visits skip the picker but it's reachable mid-session by tapping the avatar in MenuScene's top-left corner.

### Story 4 (✅ SHIPPED): Player hero selection — 4 diverse hero options

**Type:** Design + code + assets

**Context:** Let the player pick their hero from **4 options** chosen for representation — one each of: female / dark-skinned, female / light-skinned, male / dark-skinned, male / light-skinned. Every kid should be able to see themselves in the character they play. Fits this sprint because hero-select is a front-of-game presentation beat alongside the splash + intros.

**Design questions to settle first** (the games' current heroes are not all the same KIND — this needs a decision before building):
- **Which hero does the choice drive?** Number Climb's climber is a human character (today a procedural placeholder) — the 4 diverse human heroes map onto it directly. Alien Shoot + Asteroid Field heroes are spaceSHIPS (speeders / asteroid-heroes), where a "female/male, dark/light" human selection doesn't apply. Options: (a) the selection drives the Climb climber only; (b) the 4 heroes appear as the PILOT composited into/onto the ships for the arcade modes; (c) a universal player avatar shown on menus / splash / GameOver, separate from the in-game hero sprites. Decide before building.
- **Where is the picker?** A dedicated hero-select screen (when? first run? always reachable from the menu?), or a row on an existing screen?
- **Persistence** — the choice is saved (localStorage, mirroring the audience/settings pattern) so it sticks across sessions; a sensible default for first-run.

**Output (after the design is settled):**
- 4 hero images delivered (Midjourney) covering the female/male × dark/light matrix; processed through the sprite pipeline (WebP — `hero` kind, lossless per the sprint-2.2.1 story-6 convention).
- A hero-select UI — the player picks one of the 4; the choice persists.
- The selection is applied per the design decision (Climb climber and/or arcade pilots and/or menu avatar).
- CREDITS.md entry for the 4 new hero images.
- Telemetry: which hero is chosen (useful representation-engagement signal).

**Acceptance:**
- The player can pick from 4 visibly diverse hero options; the choice persists across sessions.
- The chosen hero is reflected wherever the design decision says it applies.
- A sensible first-run default.
- CREDITS.md updated; tests stay green.
