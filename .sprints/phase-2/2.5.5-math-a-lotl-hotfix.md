## Summary

Follow-up to 2.5.4 (Math-a-lotl), which was released straight from the phone playtest. A multi-angle pass over the 2.5.4 changes found three must-fix items and a set of small should-fixes; this hotfix ships them.

## Must-fix

1. **Answer numbers hard to read near the surface.** The bubble is nearly clear, so white text sat on the light upper water at ~1.8:1 contrast (fractions shrink further). Answer labels now carry a dark outline.
2. **Last-question timeout could fire twice.** `scene.start(GameOver)` is queued, so the scene got one more update after the round ended and re-ran the timeout. The scene now locks at round end, and the countdown is a `QuestionClock` that can expire at most once per question (unit-tested).
3. **Math-a-lotl audio had no CREDITS entry.** Added ("Math-a-lotl audio", ElevenLabs, generated by the project owner).

## Should-fix (shipped here)

- A tap near the bottom edge could pick a bubble still hidden below the floor (and cost a life). Taps and number keys now only see visible bubbles (`visibleBubbles`, tested).
- A bubble the axolotl is already swimming to could float away mid-chase. The chased bubble now waits just under the surface until caught, whatever its answer.
- Copy: "Out of hearts!" → "Out of lives!" (matches the HUD's "Lives:" row); a wrong catch now shows "Oops! Try again!"; "It floated away! Here it comes again" → "Floated away! Try again!", held 1.5 s; the Q1 tap hint is removed (not just hidden) once used.
- "+points" popup gets a dark outline (it was near-invisible green on blue water) — all games.
- Axolotl: the nose tilt keeps its sense through a turn; a steering goal at the screen edge is clamped so it never paddles against the wall.
- `/assets/CREDITS.md` served `no-cache` (it was caught by the immutable `/assets/*` rule).
- Housekeeping: `pickSubsetWithCorrect` / `resolveStrike` moved to the game-neutral `roundRules.ts` (re-exported for Number Climb); magic numbers moved to `config.mathALotl`; mouth offset moved into `AXOLOTL_SHEET`; stale comments fixed; ADR-0011 dispatch table and `docs/SCORING.md` updated; art generators refuse to overwrite shipped files without `--force`; dead parameters removed; reef keys typed.

## Also shipped: infra docs for Cloudflare DNS

DNS for `mykfam.com` moved to Cloudflare (the registration stays an Azure App Service Domain). `infra/bootstrap.ps1` no longer writes to the old Azure DNS zone: it prints the CNAME target, does a read-only DNS check (warns when the record is proxied, since Azure validation needs it DNS-only), then binds and waits for Ready. `infra/README.md` gains a "DNS: Cloudflare" section (records, proxy vs. certificate validation, SSL mode Full (strict), edge cache, teardown); `infra/main.bicep` comments updated.
