## Summary

A fifth game mode, in the spirit of Space Escape: a cartoon frog has to hop across a river, one row of lily pads per question. The pads drift left and right (each row the other way); each row carries the answers, exactly one right. Land on the right pad and the frog moves up a row; land on a wrong one and it sinks — SPLASH — and the frog swims back to the row it was on (or the bank). After the last row the frog reaches the far bank and catches a fly with its tongue.

- **Hero:** one frog per round, in a random colorway (green, blue, orange). Poses: sit, blink, crouch, leap, mouth open, oops.
- **Art:** drawn in code — `scripts/sprites/frog.mjs` (frog + fly) and `scripts/sprites/pond.mjs` (water tile, eight lily pad looks, splash, banks, menu backdrop) are the source of truth for the shipped WebPs.
- **Audio** (generated by the project owner in ElevenLabs): four hop sounds (random per hop), four ribbits (right pad), three splashes (wrong pad), two "rib-bit + slurp" sounds (the fly), a river bed of a babbling brook plus crickets (two 10 s loops, layered, crickets well under the brook), and an upbeat ~20 s music loop under the ambience.

## Game rules (decided 2026-10-12)

1. **12 rows, 12 questions.** The 12th row sits against the far bank; landing it sends the frog ashore for the fly.
2. **The frog hops up the screen**; the camera follows, showing a few rows at a time. Rows drift in alternating directions.
3. **Input:** tap the pad you want (it holds still once the frog commits); keys 1–4 pick the active row's on-screen pads left to right.
4. **Wrong pad:** it sinks, SPLASH, one life lost; the frog pops back up on the last row (or the bank) and the **same question** returns with fresh pads.
5. **Time runs out:** one life lost, same question with fresh pads (no splash — the frog never jumped).
6. **3 lives per round;** the last one ends the round ("Out of lives!").
7. **Difficulty** (mirrors Space Escape's 2 / 3 / 4): Easy 2 pads · 20 s, Medium 3 pads · 15 s, Hard 4 pads · 10 s; pads drift faster as it gets harder.
8. **Scoring:** shared scoring (half points on a question that needed a retry). Stars like Space Escape — by rows crossed; reaching the far bank counts as passing.
