## Summary

A cute springtime garden. A kawaii bunny stands at the front of a vegetable patch with a row of dirt holes. Each question, round smiling carrots pop up out of the holes, one per hole, each carrying an answer. **Tap the carrot with the right answer**: the bunny hops over, pulls it up with a *pop*, and it lands in the basket. In a wrong hole, the carrot ducks and a friendly mole peeks up and waves instead: a life lost, and the same question again. The answer signs stay put on garden stakes while the carrots slowly sink back into their holes as the time runs out, so the countdown is something a young player can see. When the carrots have hidden, the time is up. Twelve carrots fill the basket.

It's the eighth game, and the first set on the ground in daylight (sky, fence, flowers). Like Penguin Slide, it takes one tap per question.

## Decisions (made for the owner, who asked for them to be made; 2026-10-17)

- **Controls:** one tap per question. Tap a carrot, or anywhere in its lane below the hint. Keyboard: ← / → choose a hole, Space pulls, and 1–5 pick a hole directly (shared `NUMBER_KEYS`).
- **Right carrot:** hop, pull (*pop*), sparkle, an arc into the basket (which bounces); points; next question.
- **Wrong carrot:** the carrot ducks and a friendly mole peeks up and waves; the bunny's ears droop: "Oops! Try again!", a life lost, the same question with a fresh row.
- **Time's up:** the carrots have sunk back into their holes; "Out of time!", a life is lost, and the same question comes back with full time. This matches Penguin Slide, Frog Splash and Space Shooter.
- **Round:** 3 lives, 12 questions. Stars by questions answered (`computeClimbStars(answered, 12)`), as in Penguin Slide.
- **Difficulty:** Easy / Medium / Hard = **3 / 4 / 5 holes**, with the shared time per question (25 / 18 / 12 s, stretched for harder math).
- **Characters, drawn in code:**
  - **Bunnies:** three colorways, one at random per round: Snowball (white, pink ears), Caramel (honey brown with a cream belly) and Lop (gray, one ear flopped). Round body, big shiny eyes, blush, a tiny nose. Poses: idle, hop, pull, munch, dizzy, cheer.
  - **Carrots:** round and stubby, two eyes, blush and a smile, leafy tops; four kinds (orange, purple, yellow, white heirloom) picked at random, so the colour never gives the answer away. The answer sits on a dark badge (the Penguin Slide pill).
  - **Mole:** round, soft brown, happy closed eyes, pink nose, a waving paw. It never looks scary.
- **Scene:** a garden drawn in code: sky with a sun and clouds, a picket fence, flowers, the vegetable patch and the basket. The holes are drawn by the scene (their number depends on the difficulty). The scenery is rendered twice: full brightness for play, and darkened as the menu backdrop (lesson from Penguin Slide).
- **Audio:** the owner's music loop (`loop-9`); the bunny's hop is Penguin Slide's hop to a fish (the owner's choice; those hops now load at boot, shared). The rest the owner makes after seeing the game (a carrot pluck is coming). Until then the other effects reuse boot-loaded sounds; every picker uses `noRepeatPicker`. Still wanted: a carrot pluck, a mole *boing* / squeak (wrong) and a "hide" (time's up).

## Design review (Story 1)

Two reviews ran before the build, and both changed the design (everything below includes them):

- **Playability (young players):**
  - The answer signs are **pinned** on garden stakes above the holes. A sign that rides down with its carrot moves while a slow reader is still reading.
  - The carrots sink on an **ease-in curve** to a "peek" depth (faces still showing), then duck in over the last 0.8 s. Sinking linearly "to the leaves" hid the faces for the last few seconds.
  - **Wrong hole:** the carrot ducks and a friendly mole peeks up and waves. No dirt in the bunny's face: waking a sleeping mole read as "you did something naughty". The right answer is the bigger show (pop, sparkle, a happy hop), so the mole is never the fun one to aim for.
  - **One outcome for a right carrot:** it arcs into the basket (no munch). Doing both left "did it eat it?".
  - The bunny stands below the lip line, so it never covers a carrot. The tap window starts under the Q1 hint and leaves out the basket; lanes are at least 170 px.
  - **"Hurry":** the leaves shiver in the last 25% of the time. The hop takes the same 260 ms to any hole. The next row comes 400 ms after the basket toss and pops up in 250 ms, with taps locked until 250 ms after that.
- **Art direction (kawaii):**
  - The cheer has both arms up; the pull has the body leaning back with paws gripping and effort marks; the eyes are 15% bigger and lower with bigger highlights; the ears are 10% shorter.
  - The lop's ear hangs behind the head. The hop has one soft shadow (the white puffs read as stray marks).
  - The hole's front is a lumpy dirt mound (it read as a bowl), and its back is a plain dark opening.
  - The basket has an arched handle, a woven front and one bow (it read as a crate).
  - The fence is shorter and softer, so the signs in front of it stand out; the flowers are dimmer; the patch is a rounded bed with short furrows (it read as a barrel).
  - The pulled carrot is tilted, with root hairs and crumbs. The mole is a lighter brown than the dirt, with a bigger waving paw.
- **After the first browser run:** the carrots (110 → 140 px), the mole and the bunny (160 → 190 px) were too small at phone size.

## Build review and playtest (Story 5)

A code review and a young-player review of the built game; fixed before it went to dev:
- **The pop-up never showed:** the clock pinned the carrots' depth on the first frame. The row now pops up first; the clock and taps start together after the input lock, so no time is lost to it.
- **Pacing:** from a right tap to the next row is about 1.3 s (`happyMs` 200, `nextQuestionDelayMs` 150).
- **The right answer is the best moment:** the basket bounces, a bigger happy hop, a brighter sparkle (normal blend; an additive white one vanished on the bright garden). The mole stays 400 ms.
- The hurry shiver is visible on a phone (7 px). The Game Select subtitle says "Tap", like the hint.
- A second hop replaces one still in the air. A time-out mid-hop keeps the "oops" pose. Impossible outcomes give the question a fresh row instead of locking the round.
- Signs fit long fractions at 5 holes. The bunny's moves and the finish spot are in config. `laneMath` has its own tests.

Played in the browser (phone-sized landscape): right, wrong (mole), time-out, out of lives, a full round to "Basket full!", pause mid-toss and mid-mole, arrows / number keys / Space, two quick hops, a time-out mid-hop, fractions on Hard, the 4 + 4 Game Select, and Game Over on the garden.

## Design

### D1. Game Select: eight tiles in a 4 + 4 grid
- Two full rows of four, the same 280 × 120 tiles as 2.7's 4 + 3 grid, so the tap targets stay the same.
- New tile: "Bunny Garden", subtitle "Tap the carrot with the right answer."

### D2. Garden, holes, row and pull
- **Holes:** 3 / 4 / 5 holes in one row across the patch (`config.bunnyGarden.patch`), equal lanes. Each hole is a dark oval with a dirt lip in front.
- **The carrots' sinking is the countdown.** Each carrot's depth comes straight from the question clock (`sinkProgress(remainingSec, totalSec)`): fully up when the row appears, sunk to its leaves when time is up. The badge rides with the carrot but never goes below the hole's lip, so the answers stay readable to the end. The picture and the timer can't disagree, and pausing freezes both.
- **What's hidden:** each carrot is cropped at the ground line (`setCrop`), so it really sinks into the hole rather than sliding in front of the dirt.
- **The pull:** the bunny hops along the front of the patch to the hole (one hop arc), then pulls; the outcome plays when it gets there. Input is locked from the tap to the next row.
- **Basket:** carrots pile into a basket at the left of the patch, one per right answer (12 = full), so progress is visible without reading.

### D3. Rules (shared, pure, tested)
- Reused as is: `pickRowAnswers`, `resolveRetryEvent`, `QuestionClock`, `laneCenters` / `laneAt` / `laneForNumberKey` / `inTapWindow`.
- New and pure: `bunnyGardenMath.ts` (`sinkProgress`, `carrotDepth`, `basketSlot`), with tests.

### D4. Reuse, config and telemetry
- `MathText` badges, `applyTextOutline` HUD, `screenBanner` / `floatNote` / `createTapHint`, `NUMBER_KEYS`.
- Every size, colour and timing in `config.bunnyGarden` (lesson from 2.7's review).
- Telemetry: `BunnyGarden.wrong` / `.timeout` / `.outOfLives` / `.finished`, game state only.

### D5. New-game checklist (ADR-0011)
`GameId`, the menu backdrop key + `GAME_BG_MAP` + `GAME_BG_SPACE_LAYERS`, the bright garden as a game-scoped key, `MusicKeys` / `GAME_MUSIC_MAP` (placeholder: a boot-loaded loop), `GAME_MIDGROUND_MAP`, `audioScopeFor`, `SceneKeys` + `gameSceneKeyFor`, `boot.ts`, the Loading caption, the tile, `speedDisplayFor` + `speedSectionTitleFor` ("3 holes · 25s"), the `SpriteKey` union, `mathTimeScale.test` / `extendChoices.test`, `MathText` labels.

### D6. Art (drawn in code; new filenames; budgets)
- `scripts/sprites/bunny.mjs` → `hero/bunny-{snowball,caramel,lop}.webp` (6 poses each, ≤ 40 KB each).
- `scripts/sprites/carrot.mjs` → `hero/garden-carrots.webp` (4 kinds × 2 frames: up, pulled) and `hero/garden-mole.webp` (2 frames: pop, blink), ≤ 40 KB together.
- `scripts/sprites/garden.mjs` → `bg/bunny-garden-patch.webp` (game) and `bg/bunny-garden.webp` (darkened menu backdrop), ≤ 60 KB each.
- All game-scoped (`game:bunny-garden`) except the menu backdrop.
