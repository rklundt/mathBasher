## Summary

The Puzzles subject gets three groups: **Visual** (the eight puzzles there today), **Words** and **Numbers**. Eleven new puzzles: Words 1–5 and Letter Garden, Numbers 1–5. Every new puzzle plays in two looks, **Animal Pals** (kawaii and fantasy animals) and **Alien Invaders** (aliens and robots), with the same gameplay underneath; the look is picked on the puzzle's setup screen for now. The other nine ideas wait in [the puzzle backlog](../puzzle-backlog.md).

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-01)

1. **Build:** Words 1–5 (Word Train, Odd Word Out, Word Wand, Missing Letter, Word Snap), Numbers 1–5 (Number Train, Number Machine, Odd Number Out, Make Ten, Quick Count), and Words 10, Letter Garden ("as long as we can make this capability modular and reusable": the drag-through-letters input is its own piece).
2. **Backlog:** Words 6–9 and Numbers 6–10 go in a backlog file to come back to.
3. **Groups:** Puzzles › Visual / Words / Numbers. "Visual" holds Pair Pals and the seven puzzle-pack puzzles.
4. **Looks (themes):** every new puzzle has a kawaii animal + fantasy animal look and an alien / robot look, "different images and music/sounds, with same back end game play". How a look is picked comes later; for now, ask on the puzzle's setup screen.
5. **Timers and points:** a countdown on each puzzle where it makes sense, scored like the puzzles today: a wrong first try earns less (yellow), a quick right answer earns a speed bonus ("Earns less is what I meant by lose points").
6. **Word pictures:** drawn in code now; the owner may supply a picture sheet later (with the sounds) as a fast follow, so the pictures sit on a sheet whose frames can be swapped.
7. **Letter Garden's word list:** a big list of everyday American English words (SCOWL sizes 10–35), filtered for kids. Almost any real word a kid finds counts.
8. **Letter Garden's round (2026-11-01):** "8 words, timer each": eight words a round, sand for each word, a new garden every 4 words; green / yellow / red and stars as in every puzzle.
9. **The looks' names (2026-11-01):** "Animal Pals / Space Invaders (assuming that phrase is not copyrighted)". Space Invaders is an arcade game's registered trademark, so the space look is **Alien Invaders** (one line in `core/puzzleThemes.ts` to change).

## Stories

1. Groups: Puzzles › Visual / Words / Numbers on the Game Chooser (trail, Back, remembered group, Change Game).
2. Looks: a theme kit per look (backdrops, dancers, music, right / wrong sounds, colours, stage props); the setup screen's "Pick a look!" row, remembered per puzzle.
3. The word and number "pick one" puzzles (one scene): Word Train, Odd Word Out, Word Wand, Missing Letter, Word Snap, Number Train, Number Machine, Odd Number Out, Quick Count.
4. Make Ten: tap the cards that add up to the target.
5. Letter Garden: drag through touching letters to make words, with a reusable drag-path input (and typing on a keyboard).
6. Art drawn in code: the picture sheet, both looks' dancers, backdrops and props, the new tile pictures.
7. The word list: built from SCOWL by a script, filtered, credited.
8. Tests for every generator (each puzzle has exactly one right answer), the assets, the groups.

## As built

- **Groups:** `core/gameGroups.ts` has the puzzles' groups (`PUZZLE_GROUPS`: Visual, Words, Numbers) beside the math ones; a group knows its subject, so `chooserSteps` gives Puzzles a group step, Change Game opens the right group, and the trail reads Puzzles › Words. Three groups sit three across (`layout.chooser.groups3`).
- **Ids:** `VisualPuzzleId` (the puzzle pack), `WordPuzzleId`, `NumberPuzzleId`; `RoundPuzzleId` is all of them. The puzzle pack's own maps (backdrops, icons) are keyed by the visual ids only.
- **Looks:** `core/puzzleThemes.ts` (ids, names); `spriteKeys.PUZZLE_THEME_ART` (backdrops, dancers, train, machine, wand or zapper, counters); `audioKeys.PUZZLE_THEME_SOUNDS` (music, right / wrong sounds; stand-ins borrowed from Pair Pals and Alien Shoot); `ui/puzzleThemeLook.ts` (stage colours). `ThemedRoundScene` reads the look from `PuzzleProgress.theme` and hands it to the shared round frame (`PuzzleRoundScene.look()`); the puzzle generators never see it. Both looks' art loads with any of the eleven puzzles (about 0.5 MB, once a visit).
- **Scenes:** `ThemedPickScene` (the nine pick-one puzzles: train, shelf, wand, machine, missing letter, snap, flash stages), `MakeTenScene`, `LetterGardenScene`. The setup screen adds "Pick a look!" for them.
- **Generators (pure, tested: each puzzle has exactly one right card):** `systems/puzzles/wordPuzzles.ts` and `wordData.ts`, `numberPuzzles.ts`, `letterGarden.ts`. Word Snap Hard is checked against the big word list so no wrong half makes a real word.
- **Drag input:** `ui/dragPath.ts`, reusable for any grid-tracing puzzle (it knows cells and a "may follow" rule, not letters).
- **Word list:** `scripts/words/build-word-list.mjs` (`pnpm words:build`) writes `public/assets/words/letter-garden-1.json`: about 24,000 words of 3–8 letters (7,500 common), and 240 checked boards per level (enough common words, nothing on the blocklist traceable, no Q / X / Z / J planted). The blocklist is ROT13-encoded so it doesn't read as plain text in the repo; the browser never gets it. Credited in `public/assets/CREDITS.md` with SCOWL's notice.
- **Art:** `scripts/sprites/themes.mjs` (`pnpm sprite:themes`): 50 word pictures (`puzzle-pics.webp`, 128 px, 10 across), both looks' dancers (6 characters × idle / happy / oops), 4 backdrops per look, the rocket train, the machines, the counters, the eleven tile pictures, the two setup backdrops. The owner's picture sheet can replace `puzzle-pics.webp` frame for frame (see the list in `wordData.PICTURE_WORDS`).
- **Checked in the browser (1280 × 720):** the groups, the Words list, a setup screen, every new puzzle in at least one look and level (both looks across them), a right and a wrong answer in Quick Count and Make Ten, a real mouse drag and typed word in Letter Garden, the reveal, a whole round to the end panel, and Change Game to Puzzles › Numbers.

## Playtest fixes (owner, 2026-11-01, after v2.16.0)

1. **Pictures:** "it's a bit confusing that some cards have images and some don't": a row of cards shows pictures only when every card has one.
2. **Stars:** a round of all right answers, a few after the halfway notch, got ★. Owner's pick: yellow counts half. Stars come from green + ½ yellow (`config.puzzles.stars`: ★★★ from 7, ★★ from 5); the best stars are kept per puzzle and level (`PuzzleProgress.bestStars`); the end panel says "5 green and 3 yellow!". Every puzzle, Visual too.
3. **Missing Letter:** "hard can miss 2 letters… need more variety (I got train twice — use that big word list)": `wordData.KID_VOCAB` (about 290 kid words) besides the picture words; a word without a picture is used only with the big word list loaded (shared with Letter Garden) and every wrong card checked not to spell a real word; Hard is 5–7 letters with two gaps, filled one letter at a time in any order from seven single-letter cards (owner: "2 separate letters (2 choices)… another option or two"; `missingLetter.cards`); the two missing letters always differ; no wrong letter spells a real word in either gap; a round never repeats a word. A puzzle can have several right cards (`ThemedPuzzle.answers`).
4. **Quick Count:** "more time for medium and even more for hard to count": the counters show 1.8 / 2.4 / 3.0 s.
6. **Variety and life (2026-11-01, waiting on audio):** "assure we have good variety of our sprites… make these games more animated in the background". Each look now has 10 dancers (`theme-<look>-dancers-2.webp`; the 6-character sheets retired), 6 backdrops, 8 Quick Count counters (`theme-counters-2.webp`), and flyers (`theme-flyers.webp`, `ui/themeFlyers.ts`): now and then one drifts across behind the puzzle — flutter, rise, fall, glide or streak — at most two at once (`config.puzzles.flyers`), paused with the round. Hooked in through `PuzzleRoundScene.decorate()`.
5. **Number Machine Hard ("what is the 2 steps idea?"):** each rule does two things at once (doubles it, then adds 1); three examples show it. Explained; no change unless the owner asks.
