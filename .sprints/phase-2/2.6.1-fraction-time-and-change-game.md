## Summary

Two playtest requests:

1. **Fractions need more time.** Adding and subtracting fractions takes much longer than whole-number math at every difficulty; their time multiplier goes from ×2 to ×3 (all six games: per-question countdowns, Space Escape's round timer, Alien Shoot's descent).
2. **A "Change Game" button after a round.** Game Over offered Play Again, Change Difficulty and Main Menu; switching games meant Main Menu → Start → Game Select. Game Over now has Play Again, then a row of Change Difficulty · Change Game · Main Menu. Change Game goes straight to Game Select; the math type and difficulty carry over.
