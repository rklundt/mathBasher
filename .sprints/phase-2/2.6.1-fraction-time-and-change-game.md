## Summary

Two playtest requests:

1. **Fractions need more time.** Adding and subtracting fractions takes much longer than whole-number math at every difficulty; their time multiplier goes from ×2 to ×3 (all six games: per-question countdowns, Space Escape's round timer, Alien Shoot's descent).
2. **A "Change Game" button after a round.** Game Over offered Play Again, Change Difficulty and Main Menu; switching games meant Main Menu → Start → Game Select. Game Over now has Play Again, then a row of Change Difficulty · Change Game · Main Menu. Change Game goes straight to Game Select; the math type and difficulty carry over.

### Story 2b: One shared base time for every timed game
Asteroid Field and Math-a-lotl used 25 / 18 / 12 s per question, Frog Splash 20 / 15 / 10, Space Shooter 20 / 15 / 12, and Space Escape a 250 / 180 / 120 s round timer (about 20.8 / 15 / 10 s per floor). Now one shared base, `config.round.baseQuestionSec` = 25 / 18 / 12 s (the more generous set), read through `questionSeconds()` / `roundSeconds()`; Space Escape's round timer is the base × its 12 floors (300 / 216 / 144 s). The per-game countdown fields are gone (a test keeps it that way). Alien Shoot keeps its own fall speeds (its limit isn't a clock), still slowed for harder math. ADR-0011 now says new modes use the shared base unless their limit genuinely isn't a clock.
**Output:** `src/core/{config,mathTimeScale.test}.ts`, `src/game/scenes/{AsteroidFieldScene,MathALotlScene,FrogSplashScene,SpaceShooterScene,NumberClimbScene,DifficultyScene}.ts`, `src/game/systems/{mathALotlMath,frogSplashMath}.test.ts`, `docs/adrs/ADR-0011-per-game-mode-dispatch.md`.
