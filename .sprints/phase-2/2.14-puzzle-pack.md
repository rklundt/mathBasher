# Sprint 2.14: Puzzle pack — seven IQ-style puzzles

**Phase:** 2
**Status:** In progress (on dev for the owner)
**Blocks:** Nothing.
**Depends on:** 2.13 (Released, v2.13.0: the Puzzles subject and Pair Pals).
**Estimated effort:** 6–8 days.

## Summary

Seven new puzzles for the Puzzles subject, one for each family of question found on children's ability tests (sequences, matrices, classification, analogies, spatial reasoning, working memory, processing speed). Pair Pals already covers picture memory. Owner (2026-10-26): "I think you can build all of these … go on all 7", from the approved concept wireframes.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-26)

1. **All seven:** Pattern Train, Missing Piece, Odd One Out, Magic Wand, Spin Match, Copy Cat, Symbol Hunt.
2. **Backgrounds change a bit** between puzzles, with more texture than Pair Pals' ("more noise, but not too much").
3. **Animals** beside the puzzle, waving and slowly dancing side to side.
4. **A progress bar** through the round.
5. **Sounds:** use what we have that makes sense; the owner will swap them later.
6. **More sprites than needed**, for variety.

## Design

### D1. Flow (as Pair Pals)

- The picker's Puzzles tab lists all eight puzzles (no more "Coming soon").
- A puzzle opens (through Loading) a shared setup screen: "Pattern Train: how tough?", Easy / Medium / Hard with what changes, the best for that level, Back / Start. The level is remembered per puzzle.
- Menu Start goes back to the last puzzle picked.
- Pause: Resume · Change Difficulty · Settings · Quit to Menu.

### D2. A round

- **8 puzzles a round.** No lives, no timer (Symbol Hunt has a gentle sand timer per board).
- **A wrong pick** wiggles, plays a soft sound and says "Try again!"; the puzzle stays. A puzzle right on the first try earns a gold step on the progress bar, after a retry a silver one.
- **The end:** "You did it!", "6 of 8 right first time", 1–3 stars (8 = ★★★, 6–7 = ★★, otherwise ★), "New best!" when the first-try count beats the best for that level. Play Again · Change Difficulty · Menu.
- Best (first-try count) per puzzle per level, saved in this browser.

### D3. The shared frame

- **Backdrop:** each puzzle has its own soft colours and three variants (shapes, dots, sparkles, gentle hills), cross-fading every puzzle. More texture at the edges, calm in the middle.
- **Top bar:** the puzzle name, mute, pause; **the progress bar** under it: 8 steps that fill gold / silver, the current one pulsing.
- **Dancers:** two animals from the Balance Scale crowd (bear, kitten, piglet, bunny, panda, lamb, mouse, frog) at the bottom corners, swaying slowly; they cheer on a right answer, say "Oh!" on a wrong one, change every few puzzles.
- **Prompt pill** under the bar; **answer tray** at the bottom (3 / 4 / 5 choices by level); Left / Right / 1–5 and Space on the keyboard.

### D4. The puzzles

- **Pattern Train (sequences):** a train of cars carrying a pattern; pick what goes on the "?" car. Easy: AB, AAB, ABB, ABC by colour or shape. Medium: ABCD, big / small turns, two-part tokens. Hard: two rules at once (shape repeats every 2, colour every 3), turning a bit each car.
- **Missing Piece (matrices):** a window of 3 rows, one pane missing. Easy (3 × 3): rows share a shape, columns a colour. Medium (3 × 4): each row has every shape once in a shifted order, a turn step, how many (1, 2, 3 down the rows), or big / small taking turns. Hard (3 × 4 to 6): two rules at once (see Story 6e).
- **Odd One Out (classification):** 4 / 5 / 5 on a shelf; tap the one that doesn't belong. Easy: shape or colour. Medium: size, one round shape among ones with corners (only the circle and flower count as round), how many. Hard: kinds (animals vs desserts, from Pair Pals' cards) and turned / flipped look-alikes.
- **Magic Wand (analogies):** A turns into B; make the same change to C. Easy: bigger / smaller, colour. Medium: turn, how many. Hard: two changes at once, flip, put on a hat.
- **Spin Match (spatial):** which choice is the same shape, just turned? Easy: other shapes as the wrong choices. Medium: one mirror image among them. Hard: every choice is the shape turned, only one isn't flipped.
- **Copy Cat (working memory):** 4 / 5 / 6 animals; watch them light up and make a sound in order, then tap the same order. Starts at 2 / 3 / 4 and grows by one each of the 8 rounds; a mistake shows the order again for one more try.
- **Symbol Hunt (processing speed):** find every card like the sign (same shape and colour) on a board of 12 / 16 / 20, with look-alikes on Medium and Hard, before the sand runs out (generous; running out shows them, not first try).

### D5. Art (`scripts/sprites/puzzles.mjs`, `pnpm sprite:puzzles`)

- **Tokens:** 12 shapes × 8 colours = 96 kawaii shapes with faces.
- **Spin shapes:** 16 lopsided shapes (no two the same when turned or flipped) in 4 colours.
- **Hats:** 8 accessories (party hat, bow, crown, glasses, cap, flower, top hat, headphones).
- **Pieces:** the train engine and cars (4 colours), the window frame, the shelf, the wand, the sand timer.
- **Backdrops:** 7 puzzles × 3 variants, plus a darkened copy each for setup and Loading.
- **Dancers:** the Balance Scale crowd sheet, loaded for these puzzles too (`alsoFor`).

### D6. Code

- `PuzzleGameId` gains the seven; `PUZZLES` lists all eight with their games.
- `config.puzzles`: round length, choices per level, layout, timings, per-puzzle settings.
- Pure, seeded, tested generators in `src/game/systems/puzzles/` (one per puzzle; the right answer is always unique and among the choices; choices are all different).
- Scenes: `PuzzleSetupScene` (shared), `PickPuzzleScene` (the five pick-one puzzles), `CopyCatScene`, `SymbolHuntScene`; shared frame pieces in `src/game/ui/puzzleFrame/`.
- `PuzzleProgress` service: level per puzzle, best per level.
- Sounds: right = Pair Pals' match hits; wrong = a soft one (Math-a-lotl's bubble pop "oops"); a tap = the tile flip; Copy Cat's animals = frog ribbit, then the owner's own chick peep, pig oink and whale song, then frog slurp and penguin gulp; the round's end = the bonus cheers. Music: Pair Pals' tracks, one per puzzle, loaded on demand.

### Story 4: Generators + tests (seven)

### Story 5: Scenes (pick-one, Copy Cat, Symbol Hunt)

### Story 6: Playtest
**Played in the browser (2026-10-26):** the picker with pictures; Pattern Train's setup and round; Missing Piece Easy and Hard (wrong then right); Odd One Out Hard and a full Medium round to the end panel (7 of 8, ★★); Magic Wand Hard; Spin Match Hard; Copy Cat Medium; Symbol Hunt Hard. Fixed on the way: the train popping in too big, the dancers' flat edge showing, the window crowding the prompt, notes covering the puzzle, mirrored shapes not drawn mirrored, a Copy Cat pad stuck small, the dancers drifting up on quick answers. Not yet: every puzzle at every level to the end, a phone, the owner's check on dev.
### Story 6b: Owner's playtest (2026-10-26)
- **Green / yellow / red steps** (owner: "green if first try, yellow if second, red and continue on for 3 fails"): green = right first try (and, when timed, in the first half of the time); yellow = right after one or two misses, or slower; red = three misses or the time runs out, and the answer is shown and the round moves on. Stars count the greens. Copy Cat: three mistakes on a sequence turn it red and the next one comes.
- **Timers on Pattern Train, Odd One Out and Symbol Hunt** (owner: "needs some timing … to assure they get through faster"): a sand bar per puzzle (Pattern Train 20 / 25 / 30 s, Odd One Out 15 / 18 / 22 s, Symbol Hunt 40 / 40 / 45 s).
- **Fair choices** (owner: "you can't do that" — a same-shape answer in two sizes): a wrong choice with the answer's shape and colour may differ only in what the puzzle itself changes (size, turn, how many); tested over every seed, Pattern Train and Missing Piece.
- **Menu from a puzzle** (owner: "should take us back to subject/games"): Quit to Menu on the pause card and Menu on the end panel open the game picker on the Puzzles tab, for Pair Pals and all seven puzzles (as Back from a puzzle's setup already did).
- **Copy Cat voices:** the chick, pig and whale pads play the owner's new sounds (the chick at half gain; it measures hotter than the others).
- **Plainly round** (owner: the drop has a point and the cloud two bottom corners): Odd One Out's corners rule is always a circle or flower among shapes with corners; "the others are all round" is gone.
- **Two sizes only** (owner: kids look at shape and colour, and can't tell three sizes apart): every token is big unless a rule makes it small, and small is about half; size patterns are big / small turns (big, small; big, big, small; small, small, big).
- `PuzzleProgress` tests added.

### Story 6c: Points, and the second playtest (2026-10-28)

- **Points** (owner: "we also need to report up points"; `services/puzzlePoints.ts`), measured against an Add to 10 round at the same difficulty so they follow `config.scoring`. The level's multiplier is the math difficulty's (Easy 1×, Medium 1.25×, Hard 1.5×); no challenge multiplier.
  - **The puzzle pack:** a round is worth up to half an Add to 10 round (Easy 1,200 · Medium 1,500 · Hard 1,800). A right answer is 75 / 93.75 / 112.5, halved after a miss (as in math), plus math's speed bonus (⚡ +10 %, ⚡⚡ +30 %, ⚡⚡⚡ +50 %); red earns nothing. The timed puzzles measure speed by the sand; the others against 30 s (`puzzles.points.speedSeconds`), Copy Cat 4 s per sound from "Your turn!". A finished round adds the Finish Bonus and an all-green one the Perfect bonus, both at half.
  - **Pair Pals:** a win is worth up to a whole Add to 10 round (Easy 2,400 · Medium 3,000 · Hard 3,600). Full points in at most one turn per card; every 10 % of the cards in extra turns (rounded up: 12 cards → 2 turns) takes 10 % off; never below 0. 30 cards in 33 turns: 3,240. All three numbers in `pairPals.points`.
  - Shown: "Points: N" in the top bar with a "+N ⚡⚡" pop (Copy Cat: "Sounds: 3 · Points: N"), and on the end / win panel. Counted once into "This visit" and My Scores, which now lists Puzzles under Math (a puzzle left mid-round counts as ended early).
- **Bigger "big":** big tokens grew into the card's margin (0.9 → 1.04 of the token box); a hatted shape is drawn a little smaller and lower so the hat stays on the card.
- **Magic Wand Hard is always two changes** (owner: a mirror flip alone, or a hat alone, was one): hat + colour, flip + colour, bigger + colour, turn + colour, how many + colour, smaller + hat, shape + hat. Tested over every seed.

### Story 6d: Focus group (2026-10-28)

- **Symbol Hunt Hard is harder:** 8 columns (24 cards, was 7 / 21), and every other card is a look-alike (the sign's shape in another colour, or its colour on another shape); 60 % of those are near ones: a neighbouring colour (red / orange / pink, blue / teal / purple …) or a similar shape (pentagon / hexagon, square / diamond, circle / flower …). Lists in `puzzles.symbolHunt.nearColours` / `nearShapes`; tested.
- **More going on in the background** (owner: worth a little distraction if it keeps them playing): every puzzle and Pair Pals now has a slow drift and zoom on the backdrop, soft bubbles and small stars floating up, and a twinkle about every second (`game/ui/puzzleAmbience.ts`, `puzzles.ambience`). Plain bubbles and sparkles only, never puzzle shapes or pictures, so nothing can be mistaken for an answer. The two dancers also do a little hop on their own every few seconds.
- Symbol Hunt's and Copy Cat's top-left labels share the slot with the points ("Found 1 / 3 · Points: 150").

### Story 6e: Missing Piece windows (owner, 2026-10-28)

- **Owner:** "missing piece hard should be medium maybe with an extra column - hard should probably be 4-6 columns".
- **Medium** is the old Hard, a column wider (3 × 4): every row has the same four shapes moved along one, or each one turns a quarter more (a full turn across the row).
- **Hard** is 3 rows of 4, 5 or 6 columns (picked per puzzle), two rules at once: the shapes move along one each row and each column has its colour; each one turns a little more and each row starts one turn further; or two shapes take turns and each row has one more. The two shapes that take turns are never look-alikes (no hexagon next to a pentagon when they're small).
- The old Medium rules stay in Medium's mix (owner: "put count and big/small into medium's mix"), on 4 columns: each column has its shape and each row its number (1, 2, 3), or each row has its colour going big, small, big, small. Columns in `puzzles.missingPiece.cols`; tested.
