## Summary

Owner (2026-10-22, while planning My Scores): "if a kid makes it through perhaps we can talk about a bonus for completing." My Scores now shows rounds that ended early; this sprint gives a child a reason to stay to the end: a **Finish Bonus** at Game Over for a round they made it through.

No new screens. It changes the round's score, so it touches Game Over, the "Up to N points!" lines, the high-score badge, My Scores, the "This visit" total and the scoring docs.

## How rounds end today

| Game | Lives / time | A round can end early by | "Passed" (the "Round Complete!" headline) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alien Shoot | none | — (always 12 questions) | 8+ right |
| Asteroid Field | 2 wrong shots per question, no round lives | — (always 12 questions) | 8+ right |
| Math-a-lotl | 3 hearts | running out of hearts | 8+ right |
| Space Shooter | 3 lives | running out of lives | 8+ right |
| Space Escape (Number Climb) | 3 lives + a round timer | lives or time running out | reaching the top |
| Frog Splash | 3 lives | running out of lives | reaching the far bank |
| Penguin Slide | 3 lives | running out of lives | the last row |
| Bunny Garden | 3 lives | running out of lives | the last carrot |

So "made it through" isn't the same in every game: Alien Shoot and Asteroid Field always reach question 12, even with every answer wrong.

## Proposal

### P1. Who earns it (recommended rule)

**Finish the round with at least one star**, the same moment the headline says "Round Complete!":
- the round reached its end (last question, the top, the far bank…), not cut short by lives or time; **and**
- it passed (8+ right in the arcade games; the climbing / hopping games pass by reaching the end).

One rule for every game, and it matches what the child already sees ("Round Complete!" + stars = bonus). Alien Shoot and Asteroid Field can't hand it out for 12 wrong answers. A round that ran out of lives never earns it, even with 8 right.

### P2. How big (recommended size)

**Worth 3 right answers**, scaled like everything else: `300 × challenge × difficulty`. About a quarter of a perfect round:

| Challenge | Easy | Medium | Hard |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Add to 10 (×1) | +300 | +375 | +450 |
| Multiply 10×10 (×2.5) | +750 | +938 | +1,125 |
| Subtract Fractions (×4.5) | +1,350 | +1,688 | +2,025 |

Not halved by a wrong shot (the round's answers already were). Set in `config.scoring.finishBonusAnswers` (3) so it's a one-number change.

### P3. Where it shows

- **Game Over:** the score counts up to the round's points, then **"Finish Bonus! +375"** pops in amber under it (with a cheerful sound) and the score counts on to the total. No bonus: nothing extra shown (no "you missed the bonus" line).
- **"Up to N points!"** on Difficulty includes it (Add to 10 · Medium: 1,500 → 1,875). The picker's "1.5x Points!" doesn't change.
- **High score, My Scores and "This visit"** use the total with the bonus. My Scores doesn't split it out.
- **Telemetry:** `RoundEnded` gains the bonus (a new `bonus` property name).

```
        Round Complete!
         Score: 1,875
         Correct: 11 / 12
      Finish Bonus! +375      <- pops in after the count-up
          ★  ★  ★
      ★ New High Score! ★
        [ Play Again ]
  [Change Difficulty][Change Challenge]
  [  Change Game   ][   Main Menu    ]
```

### P4. In the code

- `finishBonus(mathId, speed)` / `perfectBonus(mathId, speed)` next to `ScoreCalculator`: pure, tested; the calculator also counts first-try right answers; `maxRoundScore` adds both bonuses.
- Each game says whether the round was **finished** when it ends: `lifecycle.endRound({ finished, … })`, a required field so a new game can't forget it (ADR-0011's checklist gains a line). Arcade games: the last question was reached; the others: their existing "reached the end" flag.
- `GameSceneLifecycle.endRound` adds the bonus when finished and passed, once, before Game Over, the high score, My Scores and "This visit" see the score.
- Game Over gets the bonus as its own number (for the pop-in).
- Fixes the 2.9.2 gap on the way: the short wait after the last answer (Frog Splash, Penguin Slide, Bunny Garden) and the "Out of lives!" banner can't be paused into, so a finished round is never counted as "ended early" and the bonus can't be lost by a pause.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-23)

1. **Rule: reach the end.** Every game's round that gets to its end (the last question, the top, the far bank, the last row or carrot) earns the Finish Bonus, stars or not. Running out of lives or time, or leaving, doesn't. Alien Shoot and Asteroid Field always reach the end, so they always earn it (P1's star rule is dropped).
2. **Size: worth 3 right answers** (the recommendation; the owner said go). `config.scoring.finishBonusAnswers` = 3.
3. **Name: "Finish Bonus!"**
4. **Sound:** the owner will send one; ask when Game Over is ready for it. Until then an existing cheer stands in.
5. **Perfect Round: add it now**, worth 3 more right answers (`config.scoring.perfectBonusAnswers` = 3), on top of the Finish Bonus. **Perfect** = reached the end with every question right on the first try: no wrong pick, no time running out, no life lost (every game already marks a question that had a miss or a time-out). Add to 10 · Easy: 1,200 + 300 + 300 = 1,800.

### What changes from the proposal

- P1 becomes "reach the end".
- "Up to N points!" is the perfect round with both bonuses (Add to 10 · Medium: 1,500 + 375 + 375 = 2,250).
- Game Over pops "Finish Bonus! +300", then "Perfect Round! +300" when earned, side by side on one line under the score, each with the count-on. The stars and the high-score badge move down a little to make room.
- Telemetry: `RoundEnded` gains `finishBonus` and `perfectBonus`.

## Questions for the owner (answered above)

1. **Rule:** finish with at least one star (P1)? Or simpler, "reach the end" only (Alien Shoot and Asteroid Field would always get it)?
2. **Size:** worth 3 right answers (P2)? Or smaller (1–2) or bigger (5)?
3. **Name:** "Finish Bonus!"? (Alternatives: "Made It Bonus!", "Round Bonus!".)
4. **Sound:** borrow an existing cheer for now, or will you send a bonus sound?
5. **Extra (optional):** a separate **Perfect Round** bonus for 12 / 12 with no misses? Recommended to leave for later and see how the finish bonus feels.

### Story 1: Design review (done 2026-10-23)

Taken: "reached the end" is `RoundController.isRoundOver` (every game advances to the last question only by answering it; lives and timers end a round sooner), so no new flag from each game; "perfect" is every question right first try (every game marks a miss or time-out as `usedWrongShot`); bonuses rounded to whole points; Game Over scores with commas; pause refused once the last answer is in (`RoundController.lastAnswerIn`) or after `beginRoundEnd()` (the five "Out of lives!" banners, Space Escape's fall and "Out of time!"); the bonuses in teal (the badge is amber); pop-in order score → bonuses → badge. Noted: letting the right bubble escape in Math-a-lotl costs nothing, so the round can still be perfect; Asteroid Field's "Out of shots!" doesn't record an outcome of its own (pause stays open there until the next question).
