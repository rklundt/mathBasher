## Summary

A fourth game mode with a gentle, cute underwater theme, giving the game a softer alternative to the three sci-fi modes. A cartoon axolotl swims through a blue reef; bubbles carrying candidate answers rise from the sea floor. The kid taps the bubble with the right answer and the axolotl swims over and catches it.

- **Scene:** the top quarter of the screen is a pink-and-purple galaxy sky (the HUD and the problem sit there, as in the other games); the bottom three quarters is blue water with coral, swaying seaweed, faint background fish and tiny rising bubbles.
- **Hero:** one axolotl per round, in a random colorway — pink / dark-pink gills, gold / orange gills, or lilac (blue-grey body, purple gills). Freckled, with a 6-frame swim cycle (tail wave, gill flutter, paddling legs) plus a happy frame for a correct catch. Faces left/right by flipping and tilts its nose up/down toward where it is swimming.
- **Art:** all drawn in code — `scripts/sprites/axolotl.mjs` and `scripts/sprites/reef.mjs` are the source of truth and render the shipped WebPs.
- **Audio:** a music loop, two layered water ambience loops, three bubble-pop sounds (random per correct catch) and three underwater wrong-catch sounds (random per wrong catch; added after playtest — the shared wrong-hit set didn't fit). The music plays under the ambience; a wrong catch sounds a little louder than a right one.

## Game rules (decided 2026-10-11)

1. **Input:** tap a bubble → the axolotl swims to it and catches it. Tapping open water just swims there. Keyboard: number keys 1–4 pick bubbles left to right.
2. **Right bubble:** pop sound + sparkle, happy frame, next problem.
3. **Wrong bubble:** the bubble wobbles and pops with the wrong-hit sound; the kid loses one of 3 hearts (round-wide, like Space Escape). The problem stays up so the kid can still find the right bubble (half points, as in the other modes). Losing the 3rd heart ends the round.
4. **Right bubble escapes** (floats up to the waterline uncaught): no penalty — the wave pops at the surface and the same problem comes back with a fresh set of bubbles.
5. **Difficulty:** Easy / Medium / Hard sets the number of bubbles (3 / 4 / 4), how fast they rise once in view, and a per-question countdown (25 / 18 / 12 s, same as Asteroid Field).
6. **Countdown** (added after the first phone playtest — a question could otherwise go on forever): shown in the HUD; a re-spawned wave does NOT reset it. At zero: "Out of time!", the question is missed, next problem. No heart is lost.
7. **Bubbles enter fast** (playtest): each bubble zips up from below the floor until it is fully in view, then drifts at the difficulty's pace.
8. **Round:** 12 problems, same scoring and stars as the arcade modes. All math types.

## Stories

### Story 1: Art generators + shipped sprites
Code-drawn axolotl (3 colorways × 6 swim frames + 1 happy frame), backdrop, answer bubble, coral ×3, seaweed (4 sway frames), background fish (2 frames, tinted in game).
**Output:** `scripts/sprites/axolotl.mjs`, `scripts/sprites/reef.mjs`, `public/assets/sprites/hero/axolotl-*.webp`, `public/assets/sprites/bg/math-a-lotl.webp`, `public/assets/sprites/bg/reef-*.webp`, `package.json` scripts.

### Story 2: Audio assets
Music loop (`music/loop-5`), two ambience loops (`midground/underwater-1`, `-2`), three pops (`sfx/bubble-pop-1..3`), encoded through the standard pipeline. Per-sound playback gain so the music sits below the ambience. Midground map widened to a list so a game can layer several loops.
**Output:** `public/assets/audio/**`, `src/core/audioKeys.ts`, `src/game/services/PhaserAudioManager.ts`, `src/game/services/GameSceneLifecycle.ts`, `src/game/scenes/GameScene.ts`.

### Story 3: Game mode wiring (ADR-0011 checklist)
`GameId` + scene key + bg / music / midground rows + asset scopes + Difficulty / Loading / Game Select entries. Game Select moves to a 2×2 grid to fit four games. The space-only background layers (parallax stars, bottom darkening band) are switched per game.
**Output:** `src/services/Settings.ts`, `src/core/sceneKeys.ts`, `src/core/spriteKeys.ts`, `src/app/boot.ts`, `src/game/scenes/{DifficultyScene,LoadingScene,GameSelectScene,BackgroundScene}.ts`.

### Story 4: Math-a-lotl scene
Axolotl entity, answer-bubble entity, reef decor, scene with the rules above. Pure helpers (wave building, lane layout, facing angle, escape check) unit-tested.
**Output:** `src/game/scenes/MathALotlScene.ts`, `src/game/entities/{Axolotl,AnswerBubble}.ts`, `src/game/systems/{ReefDecor,mathALotlMath}.ts`, `src/game/systems/mathALotlMath.test.ts`, `src/core/config.ts`.

### Story 5: Docs + playtest
