## Summary

The owner asked whether every game has a "Change Game" button on both the pause menu and the end-of-round screen. Checked in the browser for all six games:

- **Game Over:** Play Again, Change Difficulty, **Change Game**, Main Menu in every game (since 2.6.1). Nothing to change.
- **Pause menu:** only Resume, Settings and Quit to Menu, in every game. "Change Game" was missing.

The pause menu now has **Change Game**, between Settings and Quit to Menu. Like the Game Over button, it goes straight to Game Select and keeps the math type and difficulty. As with Quit to Menu, the round is abandoned and no score is saved.

## Stories

### Story 1: "Change Game" on the pause menu
- **Destination:** `GameSceneContract.quitToMenu(destination?)` takes `'menu'` (default) or `'game-select'` (the `QuitDestination` type), and each of the six games passes it to `GameSceneLifecycle.quitToMenu`.
- **Lifecycle:** it starts Menu or Game Select, and `RoundAbandoned` telemetry records the destination in `sceneKey`.
- **Wiring:** `HudScene` hands the destination from the overlay to the game. `PauseOverlay` gains the button and moves its title and stack a little to fit four buttons.
- **Checked in the browser, all six games:** the pause menu shows Resume, Settings, Change Game and Quit to Menu. Change Game lands on Game Select with the math type and difficulty kept, and nothing else left running. Quit to Menu still goes to the menu.

**Output:** `src/game/scenes/{gameSceneContract,PauseOverlay,HudScene,GameScene,AsteroidFieldScene,NumberClimbScene,MathALotlScene,FrogSplashScene,SpaceShooterScene}.ts`, `src/game/services/GameSceneLifecycle.ts`.

### Story 2: Docs + playtest + close
