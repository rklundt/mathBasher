## Summary

Owner (2026-10-24): reward answering quickly. The owner's first idea was a penalty (fewer points the longer a question takes, down to 10%); after talking it over, the owner chose a **bonus**: a right answer always earns its full points, and a quick one earns extra on top.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-24)

1. **A bonus, not a penalty.** A right answer keeps its full points; speed adds to them. Speed pressure is a known trigger for math anxiety, and a slow, careful right answer shouldn't feel like losing.
2. **Free thinking time.** Every answer in the first part of a question's time counts as fast (top tier); the bonus only separates quick from slow, it doesn't punish reading the question.
3. **Tiers kids can see**, ⚡⚡⚡ / ⚡⚡ / ⚡, shown in the "+points" popup ("+100 ⚡⚡⚡ +50"), not ten invisible 10% steps.
4. **Fair timing in every game:** the clock for the bonus starts when the answers can be tapped, not when the question first shows (Bunny Garden's and Penguin Slide's input lock, lily pads and bubbles arriving).
5. **Space Escape** gets a per-floor share of its round timer for the bonus, so its points per question stay in line with the other games. The round timer itself doesn't change.
6. **Perfect Round doesn't need speed.** It stays "every question right first try".

## How each game times a question today

| Game | The time limit | Speed measured as |
|---|---|---|
| Alien Shoot | the alien falling to the hero (fall speed by difficulty) | how far the alien has fallen when it's hit |
| Asteroid Field | a countdown per question | the countdown used |
| Math-a-lotl, Space Shooter, Frog Splash, Penguin Slide, Bunny Garden | `QuestionClock`, `questionSeconds` (25 / 18 / 12 s × the challenge's time stretch) | the clock used, from when the answers can be tapped |
| Space Escape | one round timer (the same base × 12 floors) | each floor's share: the per-question time, from when the floor's rungs are ready |

## Proposal (for the owner to confirm)

### P1. Tiers

Share of the question's time used when the right answer lands:

| Tier | Time used | Speed bonus (of the answer's points) |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡⚡⚡ Lightning | first 25% (the free thinking time) | +50% |
| ⚡⚡ Quick | up to 50% | +30% |
| ⚡ Speedy | up to 75% | +10% |
| — | after that | nothing extra |

Add to 10 · Easy (25 s a question): an answer in 6 s earns 100 + 50; in 12 s, 100 + 30; in 18 s, 100 + 10; after that, 100. In `config.scoring.speedBonus` so the cut-offs and sizes are a one-line change.

### P2. Rules

- **Only the first try earns it.** A right answer after a miss or time-out already earns half, and its clock restarted; no speed bonus there (simple to explain: "fast and right first time").
- **Not part of the round-end bonuses.** The Finish Bonus and Perfect Round stay "3 right answers' worth" each, not counting speed.
- **"Up to N points!"** grows to include a Lightning answer for every question: Add to 10 · Easy 1,800 → 2,400 (12 × 50 more). It counts in the high score, My Scores and "This visit" like any point.
- **Game Over** adds one line under Correct: "⚡ Speed bonus: +450" (only when there is one).

### P3. In the code

- A pure, tested `speedTier(fractionUsed)` / `speedBonus(points, tier)` next to `ScoreCalculator`; `QuestionOutcome` gains the fraction of the time used; the calculator adds the bonus and keeps a running speed total for Game Over.
- Each game reports the fraction with its right answer (most read their `QuestionClock`; Alien Shoot its fall distance; Asteroid Field its countdown; Space Escape a per-floor clock started when the rungs are ready).
- HudScene's "+points" popup shows the tier.
- ADR-0011's checklist: a new game reports the fraction used.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Sizes (P1):** +50% / +30% / +10% for the first quarter / half / three quarters? (Bigger makes speed matter more; smaller keeps right answers the main thing.)
2. **After a miss (P2):** no speed bonus on a retry, as proposed?
3. **Game Over line (P2):** show "⚡ Speed bonus: +450"?
4. **Names:** "Lightning / Quick / Speedy" in the popup, or just the ⚡ marks?
