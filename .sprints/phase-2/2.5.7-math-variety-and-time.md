# Sprint 2.5.7: Fairer question mix + more time for harder math

**Phase:** 2
**Status:** Closed

## Summary

Player feedback after v2.5.6, all of it shared by every game mode (the math and the time limits come from the same place for all five):

1. **Time limits ignore the math.** Fractions got the same 10–25 s per question as "2 + 3".
2. **Add to 20 had too many single-digit answers** (2 + 2, 2 + 5): every sum 2–20 was equally likely and both numbers stayed ≤ 10, so 42% of answers were single digits.
3. **Subtract within 20 wasn't random enough** — lots of "− 10" / answer 10, and a third of questions were "− 0" or "x − x" (answers were drawn evenly, so 20 could only be "20 − 0", 19 "19 − 0" or "20 − 1"…).

A measurement pass over every whole-number type found the same pattern in Subtract within 10 (about half "− 0" / "x − x") and milder easy patterns in multiply / divide (× 10, × 11).

## Decisions (2026-10-12)

- Time scaling applies to **all five games**: per-question countdowns (Asteroid Field, Math-a-lotl, Frog Splash), Space Escape's round timer, and Alien Shoot's descent speed. Multipliers: Add / Subtract to 10 ×1.0, to 20 ×1.25, Multiply / Divide / Mixed ×1.5, Fractions ×2.0. The Difficulty screen shows the real seconds for the chosen math.
- **Add to 20 allows teen addends** (12 + 5, 14 + 6) alongside crossing-ten facts (8 + 7); single-digit answers about 1 in 8.
- The variety pass covers **all whole-number math types**; fractions unchanged.

## Stories

### Story 1: Weighted question tables
Each whole-number generator lists every valid problem once and draws from a weighted table: answer weights set how often each answer comes up, problem weights pick among problems with the same answer (favour 13 − 5 over 13 − 0, 8 + 7 over 10 + 5). Tuning in `config.questionMix`.
- Add to 10: answers 2–10 even, "+ 0" rare. Add to 20: single-digit sums ~13%, crossing ten favoured, "+ 1" / "+ 10" rare, no "+ 0".
- Subtract within 10: "− 0" / "x − x" ~12% (was ~52%). Subtract within 20: answers spread over 1–18 with none above ~7%, "− 0" / "x − x" ~2% (was ~34%), mostly from a teen number.
- Multiply / divide: × 10 (and × 11 in 12×12) about half as often.
- Add / Subtract to 20: wrong answers within ± 6 of the right one.

### Story 3c: Pre-release review fixes
- Add to 20 wrong answers may go a little past 20 (up to 24), so a sum of 19 or 20 isn't always the biggest choice.
- Answer 2 in Add to 10 (almost always 1 + 1) and answer 9 in Subtract within 10 (almost always 10 − 1) come up about half as often, so no single problem tops the list; a test caps every problem at 7%.
- Space Escape's round timer shows minutes and seconds from 2 minutes up ("3:00 timer", "8:20 timer" — exact, never rounded up), not "500s timer".
- Wrong-answer time penalties stay fixed on purpose (documented in config). `pickWeighted` refuses an empty table; the Difficulty screen clears its per-visit maps; direct tests for near-answer wrong choices.
- Docs: DeveloperGuide and the ADR-0011 new-game checklist mention the time scale; PLAYTEST wording.
