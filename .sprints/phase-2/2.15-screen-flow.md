# Sprint 2.15: New screen flow — Title Screen, Game Chooser, setup screens

**Phase:** 2
**Status:** Closed — Released 2026-10-30 (v2.15.0). Wireframes approved by the owner on 2026-10-30.
**Closed:** 2026-10-30
**Blocks:** Nothing.
**Depends on:** 2.14 (Released, v2.14.0: the puzzle pack).
**Estimated effort:** 4–6 days.

## Summary

mathBasher is no longer only Math Basher, so the screens before and after a game are rebuilt around **what kind of thing you want to play**. Owner (2026-10-29): the first screen is the "Title Screen" with Play and Settings; Play opens the subject (Math, Puzzles, more later), then the subject's groups when it has them (Math: Math Basher and Math Fun), then the games; picking a game opens its type and difficulty screen. "We are reverting to the previous screen flow": the math type is picked per game again, after the game. The end and pause screens go back to the Game Chooser, the type and difficulty screen, or Settings; "I'm not sure we have any reason to ever go back to the main menu." The avatar is paused for now.

The owner approved detailed wireframes of every screen (2026-10-30), including the wording on the tiles ("New wording is MUCH better!!!").

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30)

1. **Title Screen:** the game's name, a short line, **Play** and **Settings**, the splash art, mute. No other buttons.
2. **The Game Chooser** is one set of steps: subject › group › games. A "where you are" trail of chips under the title; tapping a chip goes back to that step.
3. **A step with one choice is skipped:** Puzzles has no groups, so it opens its games straight away.
4. **"More Coming Soon"** stays on the subject step as a tile that can't be tapped ("keep it… we'll use it").
5. **My Scores** moves to a trophy button in the chooser's top bar (every chooser step), beside Settings and mute.
6. **Back walks up one step:** setup › games › group › subject › Title Screen. Change Game (pause or end) lands on the last games list.
7. **Remembered picks, already selected,** so Start is one tap: the math type is shared by every math game (a child practising Add to 20 keeps it when switching game); the speed is per game; a puzzle's level is per puzzle (as now); Pair Pals' cards and size (as now). The last game played gets a **"Last Played"** badge.
8. **Setup screens:**
   - **Math:** "What math?" (the 11 types, each with its points under it: "1.5x Points!") and "How fast?" (the game's own three speeds, each with "Up to N points!" under it, which follows the type picked; fractions keep their level notes).
   - **Puzzles:** "How tough?" (Easy / Medium / Hard with what changes, and "Up to N points!" under each).
   - **Pair Pals:** "Which cards?" (the five sets, each showing a sample picture) and "How tough?" (pairs and grid, "Up to N points!" under each).
   - Each shows the best for the current pick and a big Start.
9. **Pause:** Resume, Change Game, the game's own Change button, Settings. **End of round:** Play Again, then the same three. No Main Menu on either.
   - The Change button says "Change Math or Speed" for math games, "Change Difficulty" for puzzles, "Change Cards or Difficulty" for Pair Pals.
10. **Avatar paused:** the first-visit hero picker is skipped, and the corner avatar is gone from the menu, the in-game top bar and the end screen. The code stays, switched off (one flag), and a chosen avatar stays saved. Space Escape's robot skin (Settings › Game) is separate and unchanged.
11. **Title art and line:** the current splash stays for now, with "Math and puzzle games" in place of "Math, but with aliens." New art can come later.
12. **"mathBasher" is one word** wherever the group is named (tile, trail, tab), and the tile name never wraps (owner, 2026-10-30).
13. **Livelier tile wording** (as in the wireframes): the groups ("Aliens or animals?", "Blast aliens with your brain power!", "Hop, splash and slide with animal pals!"), and one line per game and puzzle tile ("Ribbit! Hop the right lily pads across the river.", "Choo choo! What rides next?" …).
14. **A screen-name tag** in the bottom-left corner of every screen except during play ("DEV · TitleScene", "DEV · GameChooserScene · math groups" …), so the owner can name screens while we work. Shown when running locally and on dev; never on prod.

## Design

### D1. The flow

```
Title Screen ── Play ──▶ Game Chooser: subject ──▶ group (Math only) ──▶ games ──▶ setup ──▶ Loading ──▶ game
     │                        ▲  Back                ▲  Back              ▲            │
     └── Settings             │                      │                    │            ├─ pause: Resume · Change Game · Change … · Settings
                              └──────── Back ────────┴──── Back ──────────┘            └─ end:   Play Again · Change Game · Change … · Settings
```

- Change Game opens the chooser on the games list the game is in; Change … opens that game's setup with the current picks selected.
- Settings opens over the current screen and returns to it (as today, `SettingsScene` with `onBack`).
- My Scores opens from the chooser's trophy and returns to the step it was opened from.
- Esc does what Back does on every screen.

### D2. Screens and scenes

| Screen | Scene | Replaces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title Screen | `TitleScene` | `MenuScene` |
| Game Chooser (subject, group, games) | `GameChooserScene`, one scene with a `step` | `ChallengePickerScene`, `GameSelectScene` |
| Math setup | `MathSetupScene` | `DifficultyScene` |
| Puzzle setup | `PuzzleSetupScene` (restyled) | — |
| Pair Pals setup | `PairPalsSetupScene` (restyled) | — |
| Pause | `PauseOverlay` (new buttons) | — |
| End of round | `GameOverScene` (new buttons), the puzzle end panels | — |

- One scene for the chooser keeps the trail, Back and the top bar in one place; each step is a layout of tiles. Subjects and groups come from `core/subjects.ts` and `core/gameGroups.ts`; a group with one member, or a subject with one group, is skipped.
- The setup screens share the chooser's top bar (Back, title with the game's picture, My Scores, Settings, mute) and its dark look.
- Retired scenes are deleted, not left unused, with their tests replaced. `HeroChooserScene` stays (behind the flag in D4).

### D3. Remembered picks

- `Settings` keeps the math type (`challengeId`, already shared), the speed per math game (new: `speedByGame`), the last game, and the chooser's last subject and group, **saved in this browser** (new: today the math type and speed are in memory only and reset on a reload, so there is nothing to migrate; owner, 2026-10-30). Puzzle levels and Pair Pals' picks already stay in `PuzzleProgress` and its settings.
- "Last Played" = the last game started from a setup screen.

### D4. Flags

- **`features.avatar`** (`config.ts`, `false`): Boot skips the hero picker; Title, HUD and end screen draw no avatar.
- **The screen-name tag:** shown when `import.meta.env.DEV` or `VITE_SHOW_SCREEN_NAMES === 'true'`. `deploy.yml` sets `VITE_SHOW_SCREEN_NAMES=true` only for the `development` branch, so a prod build never has it. One helper, `addScreenTag(scene, name)`, called by each menu scene; game scenes don't call it.

### D5. Points labels

- Reuses `pointsMultiplierLabel`, `upToPointsLabel`, `puzzleRoundMax` and `pairPalsMaxPoints`, so the labels can't promise more than the scoring pays.
- "Up to 4,501 points!" (Add to 20, Medium) is exact: each bonus is 562.5 rounded up to 563. Left as is unless the owner wants bonuses rounded so every maximum is a round number (a scoring change, not a label one).

### D6. Docs

- **ADR-0015:** the game-first flow (supersedes ADR-0014's part 1, the challenge-first order; its part 2, subjects and challenges in code, stands).
- `DeveloperGuide.md` (the flow), `src/game/PLAYTEST.md` (the screens to walk).

## Stories

### Story 1: Flags and the screen tag
`features.avatar`, `addScreenTag`, the `deploy.yml` variable, tests for the tag rule.

### Story 2: Title Screen
`TitleScene`; Boot routes to it (no hero picker while the avatar is paused). Play → chooser; Settings; mute.

### Story 3: Game Chooser
`GameChooserScene` (subject, group and games steps; trail; skip rule; "More Coming Soon"; Last Played; My Scores trophy; Back and Esc). The tile wording in config.

### Story 4: Math setup
`MathSetupScene`: type chips with points, the game's speeds with "Up to N points!", fraction notes, best line, Start; per-game speed with migration.

### Story 5: Puzzle and Pair Pals setups
Restyle both to the shared top bar and look; "Up to N points!" under each level; Pair Pals' sample pictures kept.

### Story 6: Pause and end of round
New buttons on `PauseOverlay`, `GameOverScene`, the puzzle end panel and Pair Pals' win panel; `quitToMenu` destinations become the chooser and the setup.

### Story 7: Avatar off, old scenes out
Hide the avatar (HUD, end screen); delete `MenuScene`, `ChallengePickerScene`, `GameSelectScene`, `DifficultyScene`, the challenge chip and the pause card's challenge buttons; update scene registration, telemetry route names and tests.

### Story 8: Playtest
Every path in D1 with mouse, keyboard and touch; 1280 × 720 and a phone in landscape (844 × 390); the tag on local and dev, not in a production build.

### Story 9: Docs + close
ADR-0015, DeveloperGuide, PLAYTEST, VERSIONS, sprint plan.

## Definition of done

- **Checks:** format check, lint, typecheck, tests and build pass; leak scan clean.
- **Playtest:** every screen and every Back / Change path; phone-sized screen and desktop; the owner's check on dev.
- **No regressions:** every game and puzzle starts, pauses, ends and replays as before; scores, bests and My Scores unchanged.
- **Release:** only after the owner's go.
